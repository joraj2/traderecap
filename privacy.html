Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 April 2026
Publisher: Jora Nagra (sole developer, New Zealand)
Contact: support@edgebook.app

This privacy policy explains what information EdgeBook ("the app", "we", "us") collects when you use our Android application, and what we do with it. We try to keep this short and clear because your trading data is sensitive and you deserve to know exactly what happens to it.

1. The data you create stays on your device

Every trade, watchlist item, pattern, screenshot, review note, mistake tag, and pre-market plan you create in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not have a server that receives or stores your trade data. We cannot read your trades. We cannot sell your trades. We cannot recover them if you uninstall the app or wipe your device.

If you want your data on another device or as a backup, use the Export function inside the app to download a JSON file. You control where that file goes.

2. What we do collect

We use Google AdMob to show advertisements inside the app. AdMob may collect:

  • A non-permanent advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) that you can reset or delete at any time in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Coarse device information (device model, OS version, language, country at country-level granularity).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (used by AdMob to comply with regional ad rules — not stored by us).
  • Whether you tapped or viewed an ad, for ad measurement.

This data is collected directly by Google AdMob and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any account information (the app has no sign-up).
  • Your trade prices, P&L, screenshots, or strategy notes.
  • Your precise location.
  • Your contacts, photos library, or other apps you use.

3. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may collect anonymous crash logs (stack traces) to help us fix bugs. These do not contain your trade data. You can opt out via Android Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. AdMob is configured to request non-personalized ads where applicable.

5. Your choices

  • Reset advertising ID — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Delete all your data — In-app Settings → Reset all data. This wipes everything from your device.
  • Uninstall — Removing the app removes all your local data.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your trade data with anyone, because we do not have it. Anonymous ad data flows from your device to Google AdMob as described above.

7. Security

Because your trade data lives only on your device, securing your device (PIN/biometric lock) is the most important step you can take. The app does not encrypt local storage beyond what Android provides at the OS level.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version at the URL where you found this page and update the Effective date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@edgebook.app

This policy is provided in plain language and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you operate this app in your own jurisdiction you may need to add jurisdiction-specific clauses (GDPR rights for EU users, CCPA for California, etc.).
